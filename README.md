# 🚀 PrepNova

### 🤖 AI Mock Interview & Interview Evaluation Platform

<p align="center">
  <strong>Practice Smarter. Improve Faster. Interview with Confidence.</strong>

<p align="center">
  PrepNova is an AI-powered platform designed to help students prepare for technical, aptitude, and HR interviews through realistic mock interviews and personalized performance evaluation.
</p>

---

## 📌 About The Project

Preparing for an interview is not only about having technical knowledge. Many students struggle with confidence, communication, aptitude questions, and expressing what they know under interview pressure.

**PrepNova** aims to bridge this gap by providing students with a simulated interview environment where they can practice, receive AI-powered feedback, identify their weaknesses, and continuously improve their performance.

The platform is being developed as a **BCA 5th Semester Minor Project**.

## 🔄 Development Methodology

PrepNova follows the **Agile methodology**.

The project is divided into smaller development cycles called **sprints**. Each sprint focuses on specific features, followed by testing, feedback, improvements, and further development.

```text
Planning
   ↓
Requirement Analysis
   ↓
UI / Prototype
   ↓
Development
   ↓
Testing
   ↓
Feedback
   ↓
Improvement
   ↓
Next Sprint
```
